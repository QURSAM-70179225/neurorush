Delete your NeuroRush account

You can permanently delete your NeuroRush account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open NeuroRush and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address you sign in with (or include your NeuroRush username). We confirm your identity and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, profile, username, display name and country
  • All rounds, scores, XP, streaks, Brain Score, achievements and coins
  • Friends, friend requests, leaderboard entries, Endless Mode and coach data
  • Notification registrations

What is kept

  • Purchase records (product, order ID, date), unlinked from your account, where tax or consumer law requires them — up to 7 years.
  • Past battles still show the result to your opponent, with your name replaced by “Deleted player”.

See the privacy policy for details.

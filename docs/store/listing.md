## Full description (max 4000)

**Train your brain in 60 seconds a day.**
NeuroRush is a fast, colourful brain-training game with 8 mini-games that get harder as you get better. Every round lasts one minute. Each one trains a real skill — memory, focus, speed, logic or language — and adapts to you as you play.

🧠 **8 BRAIN GAMES**
• Memory Grid – remember the glowing tiles
• Path Recall – watch a path, then trace it from memory
• Color Clash – tap the ink, not the word
• Odd One Out – spot the icon that’s different
• Speed Math – beat the shrinking timer
• Shape Match – same shape as before? Yes or no?
• Number Sequence – crack the hidden pattern
• Word Scramble – unscramble words in English or Urdu (اردو)

📈 **YOUR BRAIN SCORE**
Take the 2-minute Brain Test to get your starting Brain Score (0–1000). A radar chart shows your Memory, Focus, Speed, Logic and Language, and progress charts show how you improve week by week.

🏋️ **DAILY BRAIN WORKOUT + AI COACH**
Every day you get 3 games picked for your weakest skills. Finish the workout and Neo, your AI Brain Coach, gives you a personal tip and a fun fact about the brain.

♾️ **ENDLESS MODE**
Fresh riddles, logic puzzles and number sequences made by AI and double-checked before you see them. How long can your run last?

⚡ **LIVE 1v1 BRAIN BATTLES**
Challenge a random player or invite a friend with a code. Both of you get the same 8 questions at the same moment — the fastest correct answers win trophies.

🔥 **STREAKS, LEVELS & 30 BADGES**
Keep your daily streak alive (streak freezes save you on busy days), level up from Rookie Brain to Neuron Master, and unlock 30 achievements like Lightning Reflexes.

🏆 **LEADERBOARDS & FRIENDS**
Weekly leaderboards for the world, your country and your friends. Add friends by username, see their streaks and beat their scores.

✨ **MADE FOR EVERYONE**
• Works offline — progress syncs when you’re back online
• Light, dark and unlockable neon themes
• Large text, colour-blind-safe colours and screen-reader support
• Sounds and haptics you can switch off

**NeuroRush Pro** (optional monthly subscription): no ads, unlimited Endless Mode, all neon themes and 30-day skill trends. Cancel anytime in Google Play.

NeuroRush is a game for fun and practice. It does not diagnose or treat any medical condition.
